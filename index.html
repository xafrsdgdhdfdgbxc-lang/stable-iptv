How to Set Up

Quick setup guides for our recommended IPTV apps. Get streaming in minutes.

RECOMMENDED

IBO Player Pro

  1. Install IBO Player Pro from your device's app store (Samsung Smart Hub, LG Content Store, Google Play, or Amazon Appstore).
  2. Open the app and write down your MAC Address and Device Key shown on screen.
  3. On your phone or computer, visit iboplayer.pro/manage-playlists.
  4. Enter your MAC Address and Device Key, then click Login.
  5. Click Add Playlist and choose M3U URL or Xtream Codes.
  6. Paste the playlist details we send you via WhatsApp, then click Save.
  7. Return to your TV, restart the IBO Player Pro app, and your channels will load automatically.

💡 IBO Player Pro includes a free trial period. After that, the app may require a one-time activation fee paid directly to the app developer — this is separate from your IPTV subscription.

IBO Player

  1. Install IBO Player from your Smart TV, Android TV, Firestick, or mobile app store.
  2. Open the app and note your MAC Address and Device Key.
  3. On your phone or computer, go to iboplayer.com/device/login.
  4. Enter your MAC Address and Device Key to log in.
  5. Click Add Playlist or Add XC Playlist and enter your M3U URL or Xtream Codes.
  6. Save your playlist, then restart the IBO Player app on your device.
  7. Your channel list, movies, and series will appear automatically.

💡 Make sure to copy and paste the playlist URL or Xtream credentials exactly as we send them. Extra spaces or missing characters will prevent channels from loading.

What internet speed do I need? +

For SD channels: 10 Mbps minimum. For HD channels: 15 Mbps minimum. For 4K streaming: 25 Mbps or higher recommended. Most home internet plans easily meet these requirements.

Can I use it on multiple devices at the same time? +

For stability reasons, one subscription code supports one device at a time. You cannot use the same account on multiple devices simultaneously. However, you can switch between different devices at different times (for example, watch on your TV during the day and on your phone at night). If you need multi-device simultaneous streaming, please contact us on WhatsApp for options.
